Checks have specific meanings.

Quality evidence should describe what happened, without collapsing every question into a single score.

StateWhat it tells you
PassedAn applicable check ran and its criterion was met.
FailedAn applicable check ran and its criterion was not met.
MissingA signal or value was unavailable; required and optional signals are distinguished.
UncheckedNo applicable check ran. This is not evidence of passing.
InvalidStructural or invalid signal data prevent a trustworthy result.
