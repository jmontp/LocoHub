In progress: Fukuchi Walking

Fukuchi 2018 Walking is the first addition. It provides participant averages for overground and treadmill walking: angles, joint moments and ground reaction forces. Version 6 includes 51 participants and 508 participant-condition pairs. These are averages, not individual strides.
