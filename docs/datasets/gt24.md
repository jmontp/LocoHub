!!! note "Erratum (2026-10-30): ankle sign"
    The publisher README (readme.txt, lines 90-91) states that ankle plantarflexion is positive. The data show the opposite: the source ankle angle, velocity and moment columns are dorsiflexion-positive, and the moments are internal (plantarflexor moment negative). Evidence, 6 seeded random subjects, 12 legs: the ankle moment matched a force-plate lever-arm estimate of the dorsiflexion-positive internal moment (ratio 0.96 to 1.11; same sign in 231 of 231 stances); level and uphill push-off ankle work was positive in 154 of 154 stances; the angle decreased into toe-off in 154 of 154 stances; toe walking (measured centre of pressure 12 to 17 cm further forward) gave a lower ankle angle than heel walking in 12 of 12 legs (two of them by under 1°). The README hip and knee statements agree with the exoskeleton encoders. LocoHub therefore does not negate the source ankle channels. Audit: `docs/quality/audits/gt24-c02-ankle-sign.json`.
