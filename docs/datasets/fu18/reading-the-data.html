Reading the walking averages

Only the small pilot has passed source checks. This guide describes the prepared full conversion; it does not announce a full dataset download.

What you selectWhat you get
Participant + condition + legOne average curve supplied by the authors. It is not one stride.
Phase150 points from 0% through 100%, linearly interpolated from 101 source points. More points do not add measured detail.
Right legOriginal components and six named sagittal angle/moment signals, using the author's right-leg labels.
Left legOriginal components. Named anatomical signals remain unavailable until their directions are verified.
A missing conditionNo observation. Coverage differs between participants; no missing curve is filled in.

Units and directions

Columns beginning with source_ retain the author's component name and units. Their values are interpolated onto the export grid; the acquired source text remains the evidence for the original 101 points. X, Y and Z are source components, not automatic labels for forward, sideways or up.

QuantitySource columnsNamed right-leg columns
AnglesDegrees, for example source_RHipAngleZ_degRadians; hip flexion, knee flexion and ankle dorsiflexion are positive. The preview displays degrees.
MomentsNm/kg, for example source_RKneeMomentZ_Nm_kgNm/kg; hip uses the source sign. Knee and ankle reverse the source sign to express flexion and dorsiflexion.
Ground reaction forcesN/kg, for example source_RGRFY_N_kgSource components only. N/kg is not body weights; do not treat a value of 9.81 N/kg as 9.81 BW.

Comparisons these files support

Compare participant averages for the same condition, leg and signal. A standard deviation across those averages describes differences between people; it is not stride-to-stride variability within a person. The files do not supply individual stride counts, stride durations or within-person standard deviations.

Left and right curves each use their own leg's cycle reference. Matching percentages do not establish simultaneous bilateral measurements. Do not subtract them as if they were synchronized samples.

Overground codes OS, OC and OF mean slow, comfortable and fast. T01–T08 identify treadmill conditions; the prepared outputs do not assign numerical walking speeds. Use verified participant-specific source metadata before making a speed comparison.

Source and changes

Claudiane A. Fukuchi, Reginaldo K. Fukuchi and Marcos Duarte: A public dataset of overground and treadmill walking kinematics and kinetics in healthy individuals (2018). The paper describes 42 participants. The pinned version 6 source contains 51 participants in the reviewed inventory.

The dataset is CC BY 4.0. LocoHub's prepared changes are table conversion, separate leg-average identities, 101-to-150-point interpolation and the explicit right-leg unit/sign changes above. Source review does not imply endorsement by the authors or validation of every anatomical component.
