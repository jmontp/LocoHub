Fukuchi 2018 Walking

First preview: what is being checked?

The small pilot passed its independent source comparison. This review covers one participant and one walking condition. A local interactive preview is ready; no public data release is available yet.

ScopeFirst pilot
Participant and taskParticipant 01, comfortable overground walking.
What one curve meansA participant’s average for that condition. Left and right are separate observations, not simultaneous recordings.
Samples101 source points interpolated to 150 points from 0 to 100%. This does not recover individual strides.
SignalsSource angle, moment and force components. Supported right sagittal angles and moments also receive standard names. Left components retain their source names.
UnavailableIndividual stride count, cycle duration, within-participant standard deviation and simultaneous timing between legs.
