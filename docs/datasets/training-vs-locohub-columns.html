Training files vs LocoHub rebuilds, column by column

What this is. For each signal the ankle model reads, how far the number in the training file is from the same sample in the rebuilt LocoHub data. Descriptive only: the “reporting sizes” (2°, 20°/s, 0.05 Nm/kg, 0.05 BW, 0.02 m) only decide which differences are counted in the last column. They are not quality limits, and nothing here approves or rejects either file. Differences are training minus LocoHub. Unreviewed.

Headline

  • Ankle moment and vertical force match. GT21 ankle moment: RMS difference 2.97e-14 Nm/kg over 5,630,728 samples; GT21 vertical force RMS 0.00333 BW. UM21 ankle moment RMS 5.64e-15 Nm/kg (the training value is read between 100 Hz frames).
  • Angles differ, and for some participants by whole turns. GT21 ankle angle averages -10.6° apart (RMS 17°), shank 2.79° (RMS 12.3°), foot -7.81° (RMS 18.1°). Where the RMS is far above the mean (for example AB24, AB07, AB16), a read-only look at AB24 found training angles near ±350° against LocoHub angles within ±30° (section 3). UM21 angles are much closer: 3.33°, -2.57°, 0.789° mean difference.
  • Velocity and centre of pressure differ by method and origin (see caveats).

4. Alignment

Training and LocoHub clocks are not assumed equal. Each training recording is matched to a LocoHub recording by name, then shifted in time by cross-correlating the ankle moment (exact moment difference over all shifts, both signs); a pair counts only if the best moment RMS is at most 0.10 Nm/kg over at least 100 samples (and 30% of the training moment samples). The tolerance only decides whether two recordings are the same recording.

GT21

  • Training recordings found in the files: 8,458; matched to a LocoHub recording: 7,886 (98.3% of training rows).
  • Largest moment difference among matched pairs: 2.02e-13 Nm/kg (alignment tolerance 0.10 Nm/kg). Sign of best fit: {'same': 7886}.
  • Reference leg chosen by best fit (the training file does not keep it): {'left': 1602, 'right': 6284}.
  • Training recordings not compared: 568 – alignment impossible: too little overlapping moment.

6. Caveats

  • Velocity in the training file is its own backward difference of the sampled angle; LocoHub uses the source or stride slope. The difference is a method difference.
  • Centre of pressure: the LocoHub value has the 0.100 m origin shift added back before comparing, so the mean difference also contains any difference between the two ankle-centre definitions. It is only present where force was measured.
  • UM21 has no LocoHub force, and no time table; comparisons use LocoHub phase samples only.
  • Angles: the training UM21 shank/foot values include its own −2× pelvis tilt correction; the training GT21 values were built on the old pelvis tilt, and the angle zero differs between the two (static pose vs gravity).
  • Coverage is the fraction of compared positions with a value in each series; a missing value in either series removes that sample from every statistic of that column.
  • Training recordings with too little finite ankle moment to align are listed, not compared; every skipped recording is counted in section 4.
  • GT21 jobs were run two participants at a time to stay under the memory cap; pooled values merge those jobs. Reconstructed from docs/quality/audits/training-compare-*.
